Tips & how to use
  • Tap the checkbox to mark 5K completed; fill Time/Notes and Location.
  • Each entry autosaves to your browser's storage. Use Export to share with leaders.
  • Add a short activity after a walk (nature journal, map reading, cleanup) to count toward badge work.
  • Print the page or export CSV/JSON for troop records. Keep permissions & safety rules on file.
